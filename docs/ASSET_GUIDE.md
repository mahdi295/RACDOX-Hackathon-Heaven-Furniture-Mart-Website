# Asset Guide

This project relies on a curated local image library for the furniture galleries, showroom visuals, leadership profile, and brand presentation. The goal is to keep image delivery reliable, accurate, and visually aligned with the luxury showroom positioning of Heaven Furniture Mart.

## Asset principles
- Use real brand photography wherever possible.
- Keep filenames descriptive and consistent.
- Prefer clean, crisp product photography with minimal clutter.
- Avoid watermark-heavy, low-quality, or generic promotional images.
- Maintain a stable asset path system for production builds.

## Asset organization
Images live in the project asset folders and are used across the site by the build asset resolver. The project keeps assets local rather than depending on external hosting for core visuals.

## What good image selection looks like
The strongest assets for this brand are:
- showroom frontage and interior scenes
- premium bedroom, dining, and sofa setups
- custom furniture detail shots
- clean compositions with no competitor branding present
- photos with consistent lighting and premium material feel

## Quality notes
The site works best when the following are prioritized:
- good composition and framing
- realistic wood and fabric tones
- consistent color grading across collections
- high enough resolution for desktop hero and gallery views
- no heavy watermarks, text overlays, or discount graphics

## Recommended additions
If the business adds new photography later, preferred sources are:
- actual showroom interior photos
- finished living room and bedroom installations
- custom office setups
- delivery and installation moments
- close-up material and detail shots

## Naming convention
Use descriptive, consistent file names such as:
- `living-04-luxury-sofa-set.webp`
- `bedroom-02-carved-royal-bed.webp`
- `showroom-exterior-storefront-current.webp`
- `md-abul-kalam-bhuiyan.webp`

This keeps folders easy to manage and reduces confusion during content updates.

## Asset handling advice
When replacing or adding new files:
1. Keep the visual style aligned with the premium furniture brand.
2. Avoid mixing generic stock imagery into real product galleries.
3. Use exact product context when describing a product image.
4. Do not use branded assets from competing businesses or unrelated marketing campaigns.
5. Validate the image after replacement in both desktop and mobile layouts.

## Final note
The website should continue to feel like a genuine showroom experience, not a generic furniture catalogue. Real product and showroom photography is the most important asset for preserving trust and premium brand quality.
