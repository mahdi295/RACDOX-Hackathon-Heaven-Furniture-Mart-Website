# Content Review

This document captures the editorial and business review process for the Heaven Furniture Mart website. The goal is to ensure the site reflects the actual showroom, brand, copy, and business context without drifting into generic or unsupported marketing claims.

## Business intent
The website is intended to represent:
- a premium bespoke furniture showroom in Chattogram
- a design-led furniture buying experience
- a strong local trust signal through real product and showroom imagery
- a simple consultation path through WhatsApp and phone contact

## Content that is aligned with the site
The copy used across the website should remain grounded in the actual business facts, including:
- Heavenly Furniture Mart brand identity
- Agrabad showroom location
- founder-led business story
- custom furniture and interior solutions
- direct customer inquiry flow

## Review checklist for future updates
Before publishing any changes, verify:
- [ ] the contact number is current
- [ ] the email is monitored
- [ ] the showroom address is accurate
- [ ] event and milestone details are still relevant
- [ ] product descriptions reflect actual inventory
- [ ] all claims remain business-appropriate and not exaggerated

## Important editorial notes
- The website should sound premium and polished, but not inflated or generic.
- Luxury positioning is best expressed through craftsmanship, materials, and detail rather than vague superlatives.
- Real showroom and furniture photography should remain the anchor of trust.
- Bespoke messaging should stay focused on tailored design, functionality, and comfort.

## Open items to confirm with the business owner
These should be reviewed before final production launch:
- current phone and WhatsApp validity
- email monitoring and response flow
- final approval of bespoke process wording
- any milestone or award updates since the current content was prepared
- the final production domain and SEO metadata

## What should remain out of the site
Avoid adding:
- unsupported discount claims
- exaggerated market leadership language
- speculative staffing or service claims
- any unverified personal or corporate references
- generic stock imagery in places where real showroom visuals should be used

## Final note
The website should feel like a polished version of the actual brand, not a generic furniture template. The editorial review standard is to keep it accurate, premium, and grounded in the real Heaven Furniture Mart showroom experience.
